# Prevention

1. Use this only for stable prevention or screening without a current acute complaint. Select one patient and request only age, relevant risks, prior screening/vaccination and contraindication context.
2. Match population, recommendation date, care setting and jurisdiction. U.S., UK, Australian and global recommendations are evidence with explicit applicability limits; no country is the default authority.
3. A catalog-only source is not evidence retrieved. If location-specific policy matters but is not executable, say that local confirmation is required rather than filling the gap from memory.
4. Organize the answer as due now, consider after clarification, not currently supported, and unknown. Explain the source and decision boundary briefly.
5. Do not turn prevention into alarm, diagnosis, sales pressure or an exhaustive checklist unrelated to the patient.
